# Inspectional Reading Summary

## Two Types of Inspectional Reading

### Systematic Skimming (Pre-reading)

- **Title page and preface** - Note scope, aim, and author's angle
- **Table of contents** - Use as roadmap to understand book structure
- **Index review** - Estimate topic range and look up crucial terms
- **Publisher's blurb** - Often contains author's own summary of main points
- **Pivotal chapters** - Focus on chapters central to the argument
- **Strategic sampling** - Thumb through, reading paragraphs here and there, always including the last few pages

### Superficial Reading

- **Read straight through** without stopping to look up unfamiliar terms
- **Focus on what you understand** rather than getting stuck on difficult passages
- **Don't use reference materials** on first pass - they impede comprehension
- **Aim for 50% comprehension** - better than 0% from getting bogged down
- **Prepare for analytical reading** - first pass enables deeper second reading

## Key Principles

### Reading Speed Strategy

- **Variable speeds** - Different materials deserve different reading rates
- **Match speed to purpose** - Not everything needs slow, careful reading
- **Skim unworthy books** - Save time for materials that merit deep analysis
- **Read difficult books twice** - Quick first pass, then careful analytical reading

### Comprehension Focus

- **Elementary comprehension** - "What does it say?"
- **Beyond speed reading** - True comprehension requires analytical skills
- **Activity over passivity** - Inspectional reading demands alertness and engagement
- **Structure before content** - Understanding organization aids comprehension
